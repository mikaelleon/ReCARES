What ReCARES is for

Safety comes first

The subdivision looks after its households before anything else. The services on this site sit beside that commitment.

No office visit required

Residents with disabilities or limited mobility can complete requests and receive certificates entirely online, in line with RA 7277.

Faster perimeter reporting

Households along the subdivision wall can report security concerns in seconds, and related reports are linked so patterns are not missed.

Everyday services

  • Visitor passes. Pre-register guests; the gate checks them against your list.
  • Vehicle stickers. Register household vehicles and carry a digital sticker.
  • Certificates and clearances. Requested, approved, and released online.
  • Announcements. Maintenance schedules, events, and safety advisories.

How to join

  1. 01Create an account with your name, mobile number, and phase, block, and lot.
  2. 02Upload a proof of residency and one valid government ID.
  3. 03The HOA Admin verifies your household remotely from the uploaded proof and ID, usually within two working days. You do not come to the office. You are notified either way.
